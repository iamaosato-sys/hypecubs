Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The children's app: no cookies

The app itself (app.html) sets no cookies and loads no analytics. It only uses your browser's storage for things the app needs to work:

NameTypePurposeKept
hypecubs.v1Local storageSaves profiles, creature levels and quests on your deviceUntil you delete it
Service worker cacheCache storageLets the app work offlineUntil updated or cleared

Information pages for grown-ups

NameTypePurposeKept
hypecubs.consentLocal storage (necessary)Remembers your cookie choiceUntil you delete it
_gaGoogle Analytics cookie (only if you Accept)Distinguishes visitors to count visits2 years
_ga_<ID>Google Analytics cookie (only if you Accept)Keeps session state2 years

We use Google Consent Mode: until you accept, analytics storage is set to "denied" and the Google Analytics script is not loaded at all.

Change your choice

You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. See our Privacy Policy.
