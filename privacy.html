Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Privacy for kids

  • Your quests, creatures and stars are saved on this phone, tablet or computer. Nobody else can see them. Not even us.
  • We don't need your real name. A nickname is fine, or no name at all.
  • There are no adverts and nothing to buy.
  • The app doesn't track you or send anything over the internet.
  • A grown-up can delete everything any time.

Got a question? Ask your grown-up to contact us.

Who we are

HypeCubs is a free movement app for children aged 6–11, provided by Entwisle Fitness, a personal training business on the Fylde Coast, Lancashire, UK. For any privacy question or request, contact us through entwislefitness.co.uk.

The children's app (app.html): no data leaves the device

Everything entered in the app (nicknames, age group, settings, creature levels, quests and "firsts") is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. We have no servers that receive it and we cannot see it. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no in-app purchases.

  • The grown-up health check is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • The app loads no analytics, advertising, social media or other third-party scripts, and sets no cookies.
  • Read-aloud uses your device's built-in speech feature. Text is spoken by your own device.
  • We do not profile children, send notifications or use nudges to keep children on screen.

We built HypeCubs to follow the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code): high privacy by default, data minimisation, no profiling, no detrimental nudges, and transparency children can understand.

The information pages (this page, the home page and the other policy pages)

These pages are written for parents and carers. If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits to these pages. This uses cookies and sends technical information such as approximate location, device and browser type, and pages viewed to Google. IP addresses are anonymised. If you click Reject or ignore the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded. Change your choice any time with "Cookie settings".

Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Google may process data outside the UK under its standard contractual clauses. See Google's privacy policy. We use Google Search Console to see how the site appears in Google search. It doesn't place cookies on your device.

Hosting

HypeCubs is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel processes basic technical data (such as IP address) to deliver the site securely. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to withdraw consent. Because the app's data is only on your device, you control it directly:

  • Export: Grown-ups → Export backup (JSON).
  • Delete one child: Grown-ups → Remove.
  • Delete everything: Grown-ups → Delete all data, or clear your browser's site data.
  • Withdraw analytics consent: Cookie settings → Reject.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. We will never add advertising or tracking to the children's app.
