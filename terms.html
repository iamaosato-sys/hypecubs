HypeCubs is a free movement app for children aged 6–11, provided by Entwisle Fitness, Fylde Coast, Lancashire, UK (contact via entwislefitness.co.uk). It is designed to be set up and supervised by a parent or carer. By using it you agree to these terms.

Free: no payments

HypeCubs is free. There are no purchases, subscriptions, adverts or hidden fees, so there is nothing to refund. If paid features were ever added, they would be for grown-ups only, clearly priced in advance, with refund terms published before anything could be bought.

Safety: for parents and carers

  • HypeCubs provides general fitness and play ideas only. It is not medical advice and does not replace advice from your child's GP, physiotherapist or other qualified professional.
  • Check with your child's GP before starting if they have a heart condition, uncontrolled asthma, a current injury, a bone or joint problem, or any condition that affects exercise.
  • Supervise your child. Stay close when they hang from a bar, use playground equipment or do anything at height.
  • Make sure there is clear space and that any furniture or equipment used (sofas, tables, bars, playground kit) is sturdy, secure and suitable for your child.
  • Stop if your child feels pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness, and get medical help if needed. Ongoing knee or heel pain during growth spurts should be checked by a GP or physio.
  • The automatic progression is a guide. You know your child best: use the Grown-ups area to adjust levels or switch on Quiet mode.
