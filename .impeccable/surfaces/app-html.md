# Surface brief: HypeCubs kids' app + parent page

Scope: app.html (kids' quests, mode: Persuade/Experience hybrid run as Persuade world) and index.html (parent landing, Persuade). Audience: children 6–11 and their grown-ups. Must not feel babyish, school-like, or generic AI-app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every quest is a walk along a waymarked trail through a map of The Lost Forest. Refuses the kids'-app default of a dashboard of rounded cards on a gradient.

OWN-WORLD: Moss-green map ground with contour lines; six creature zones in their own colours (Bear Woods deep green, Monkey Canopy teal, Kangaroo Meadow gold, Crab Shore sea blue, Flamingo Lake pink, Gorilla Ridge slate); orange dashed trail; circular waymarker discs; routed timber posts and arrow-ended plank signs with yellow routed letters. Lilita One (routed sign lettering) for display, Lexend for reading. Map-legend panels with forest-ink borders. Drawn SVG icons, no emoji icons.

STORY: The child sees their forest, sees today's trail, taps the trailhead sign, moves, and watches the map grow brighter. Parents see a crafted, safe, ad-free product.

FIRST VIEWPORT: App home: map fills ~65% of the screen, trail from the trailhead at the bottom to three numbered posts carrying today's creature discs, the Cub at the trailhead, campfire in the centre. Below: an orange arrow-plank sign START TRAIL. Landing: the same map as the hero with the trailhead sign as the primary action.

FORM: Waymarked Trail, position 5 on the grounded list, seed:7fd02cf5e35597f2. Raises: hidden Cub on every screen (from the magazine-mark world); rounds as an unbroken marker row with a moving "you are here" light (from the step-row world); per-creature zone colours (from the city-fold world). Signature interaction: the trail draws itself and the marker walks post to post.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
